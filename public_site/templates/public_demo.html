Experiment Workspace

Workflow: Configure experiment → Record measurements → Analyse → Review scientific limitations.

Public research demonstration. This interface processes temporary measurements in your browser. It does not access the private LabPilot AI database. Do not enter patient-identifiable or confidential laboratory data. Results are not validated for clinical use.

01. Experiment Information

Create a record of the sample and conditions used for the plate-count experiment.

02. Serial Dilution Plate Measurements

Each row represents an individual agar plate. Enter dilution as its denominator: 1000 represents a 10⁻³ dilution. You can add several plates at the same dilution or across different dilutions.

01. Antibiotic Susceptibility Experiment Setup

Record the organism, isolate and testing conditions before entering inhibition-zone measurements.

Measurement guidance: Record inhibition-zone diameters in millimetres. Include the disk content when available. Multiple measurements allow calculation of mean, sample standard deviation and range.

02. Antibiotic Measurement Workspace

Create a separate entry for each antibiotic. Add or remove replicate measurements within each entry. You are not restricted to three measurements or one antibiotic.
